WELCOME, DAUGHTER OF THE KING

A fellowship raised up for such a time as this

The King's Daughters is a fellowship raised up for such a time as this — a community of women awakened to their royal identity in Christ, waiting on the outpouring of the Spirit until the wilderness becomes a fruitful field. Here you will find sisterhood, sound teaching, and a place to grow into all God has called you to be.
